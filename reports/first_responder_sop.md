# FIRST RESPONDER SOP
## Case Omega - Healthcare Ransomware Investigation

### 1. Incident Identification
- Case ID: OMEGA-001
- Incident: Suspected Ransomware Attack
- Target: Fictional Hospital Network
- Date: 18 November 2026

### 2. First Responder Procedures

Step 1: Ensure safety of hospital patients and staff.

Step 2: Inform the hospital IT security team and incident commander.

Step 3: Record incident discovery time and affected systems.

Step 4: Restrict unauthorized physical and logical access.

Step 5: Photograph the computer screens, server racks, USB devices and network connections before touching equipment.

Step 6: Record system date, time, logged-in users and running processes where safe and authorized.

Step 7: Coordinate isolation of affected systems with clinical and network teams.

Step 8: Identify computers, servers, USB drives, network logs and cloud access records.

Step 9: Create forensic images using approved tools and write blockers.

Step 10: Calculate SHA-256 hashes to verify evidence integrity.

Step 11: Label and seal all collected evidence.

Step 12: Maintain Chain of Custody records for every evidence transfer.

Step 13: Perform investigation on forensic copies only.

Step 14: Prepare the final investigation report.

### 3. Precautions
- Never modify original evidence.
- Do not run unknown malware on production systems.
- Maintain patient safety and service continuity.
- Record every investigation activity.
- Preserve all evidence securely.

### 4. Conclusion
Following this SOP helps preserve digital evidence and maintain the integrity of the investigation.
